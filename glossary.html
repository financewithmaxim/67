Leitfaden Früherkennung
EU Bank Capital Regulation & Risk Management · Raiffeisen / ÖRS

Bilingual Glossary (DE / EN)

Every technical term from the ÖRS Leitfaden Früherkennung (09/2024) reconstruction, glossed in English, with a precise definition and a Module / CRR / Leitfaden reference. German nouns are capitalised. Type below to filter live.

Architektur & Aufsicht

ÖRS (Sector Risk Controlling Services / Österreichische Raiffeisen-Sektor risk service)
The sector unit that authors and runs the Leitfaden Früherkennung and computes the quarterly RTFA centrally for all R-IPS members, ensuring one comparable methodology across the Raiffeisen sector.
→ Module 1; Leitfaden front matter
R-IPS (Raiffeisen Institutional Protection Scheme) (institutional protection scheme)
The mutual support scheme under CRR Art. 113(7) that keeps Raiffeisen members solvent; it is the consolidation perimeter for the RTFA and the reference for Großkredite (related to R-IPS CET1).
→ Module 1; CRR Art. 113(7); Leitfaden Ch.7/5.9
Sektorrisikokomitee (SRK) (sector risk committee)
The governance body that reviews the early-warning results and decides on measures; recipient of the yellow/red escalation reporting.
→ Module 1/4; Leitfaden Ch.4 (Reporting)
KI-Gruppe (§30 BWG) (credit-institution group)
The regulatory consolidation group of credit institutions under §30 BWG; stille Reserven for the solo view are computed on this §30-BWG basis. Same bank, different Auslastung than solo or IPS.
→ Module 1/14; §30 BWG; Leitfaden Ch.7
Solo- / KI-Gruppen- / IPS-Sicht (solo / group / IPS perspectives)
The three consolidation perspectives in which the same institution is assessed; each yields a different Auslastung because the Deckungsmasse and the risk perimeter differ.
→ Module 14; Leitfaden Ch.7
ICAAP / SREP (internal capital adequacy assessment / supervisory review)
The Pillar-2 internal capital-adequacy process (ICAAP) and the supervisor's review (SREP); the Extremfall is the ÖRS analogue of the ICAAP economic-capital view.
→ Module 1/4; CRD; EBA SREP GL
Aufsichtsrat (supervisory board)
The board to which the RTFA methodology and results must be explained and defended; the course's pedagogical "defend to senior management" target audience.
→ Module 1; BWG governance

Risikotragfähigkeit & Szenarien

Risikotragfähigkeitsanalyse (RTFA) (risk-bearing-capacity analysis)
The quarterly analysis that compares quantified risks against available cover (Deckungsmasse) in two scenarios; the core deliverable of the Leitfaden.
→ Module 4; Leitfaden Ch.4
Risikotragfähigkeit (risk-bearing capacity)
The institution's ability to absorb losses, i.e. whether the Deckungsmasse covers aggregated risk; the property the RTFA tests.
→ Module 4; Leitfaden Ch.4
Deckungsmasse / Risikodeckungsmasse (risk-coverage potential)
The capital and earnings available to absorb losses in a scenario (free profit + free capital + stille Reserven ± excess/shortfall); the denominator of the Auslastung.
→ Module 8; Leitfaden Ch.6/8.1
Problemfall (95 %) (going-concern / problem case)
The going-concern scenario at a 95 % confidence interval; risk = unexpected loss. At 100 % utilisation a loss exceeding the Deckungsmasse is expected once every 20 years (1/(1−0,95)).
→ Module 4; Leitfaden Ch.4
Extremfall (99,9 %) (gone-concern / liquidation case)
The liquidation scenario at 99,9 % confidence over a 1-year horizon; equals economic capital, analogous to Pillar 2 / IRB. Classified as a Liquidationsfall per OeNB/FMA.
→ Module 4; Leitfaden Ch.4
Solvabilität (solvency)
The condition of holding enough own funds to remain a going concern; the Problemfall traffic lights are calibrated against CRR + P2R + buffers.
→ Module 4; CRR Part Two; Leitfaden Ch.4
Stress-Szenario (stress scenario)
A severe but plausible state used to recompute risks; the Problemfall and Extremfall are the two governing stress views, and the makro-economic GDP shock is an additional layered stress.
→ Module 4/13; Leitfaden Ch.4/5.8
Reverse-Stresstest (reverse stress test)
A test that starts from institutional failure and works back to the scenarios that would cause it; complements the forward Problemfall/Extremfall scenarios in the ICAAP context.
→ Module 4; EBA Stresstesting GL
Risikoappetit (risk appetite)
The level of risk the institution is willing to bear, expressed through the traffic-light thresholds on Auslastung (green/yellow/red).
→ Module 4; Leitfaden Ch.4
Auslastung (utilisation / capacity usage)
Aggregated risk divided by Deckungsmasse, expressed as a percentage; the key RTFA ratio that drives the traffic-light colour. Same bank shows different Auslastung across solo / KI-Gruppe / IPS views.
→ Module 4/14; Leitfaden Ch.4
Ampelung (grün / gelb / rot) (traffic-light rating)
The green/yellow/red classification of Auslastung. Problemfall: ≤90 % (CRR+P2R+buffers) green, >90 % yellow, >95 % (CRR+P2R) red. Extremfall: ≤90 % green, >90 %–≤95 % yellow, >95 % red.
→ Module 4; Leitfaden Ch.4
Redepflicht (§63(3) BWG) (bank auditor's duty to report)
The bank auditor's statutory duty to report adverse findings; reports go to bg-lg@raiffeisen-einlagensicherung.at. Legal basis §39/§39a BWG; in force 01 Jan 2024.
→ Module 4; §63(3), §39/§39a BWG
Management Summary (management summary)
The escalation report content on a yellow light: a management summary plus measures. A red light additionally requires a detailed summary with concrete steps and timing.
→ Module 4; Leitfaden Ch.4 (Reporting)
Konfidenzintervall (confidence interval / level)
The probability level at which the not-exceeded loss is measured: 95 % for the Problemfall, 99,9 % for the Extremfall. Drives the normal quantile used for scaling.
→ Module 4/5; Leitfaden Ch.4
Value-at-Risk (VaR) (value at risk)
The maximum loss not exceeded at a given confidence over a holding period; the common metric tying market, credit and liquidity risk together in the RTFA.
→ Module 4/6; Leitfaden Ch.5.5
Horizontale / vertikale Konsistenz (horizontal / vertical consistency)
The requirement that confidence levels and horizons line up across risk types (horizontal) and across scenarios (vertical), e.g. market 10-day/95 % ↔ credit-equivalent 99 %/1yr.
→ Module 4; Leitfaden Ch.4
Risikoaggregation (Korrelation +1) (risk aggregation by addition)
Material single risks are aggregated by addition (correlation = +1), a conservative choice; only within market risk is a correlation between Zinsrisiko and Credit-Spread risk considered.
→ Module 4/14; Leitfaden Ch.4/7
Diversifiziert vs. undiversifiziert (diversified vs. undiversified)
Undiversified = sign-neutral sum of single VaRs; diversified uses a correlation matrix, e.g. \(VaR_P=\sqrt{VaR_A^2+VaR_B^2+2\,\rho_{AB}VaR_A VaR_B}\). Used for FX, spread and liquidity risk.
→ Module 6/7; Leitfaden Ch.5.5/5.7

Kreditrisiko & IRB

Erwarteter Verlust (EL) (expected loss)
\(EL=PD\cdot LGD\cdot EAD\); the loss expected on average. It is NOT capital — it sits in the Deckungsmasse as excess/shortfall (EL vs. Vorsorgen) in both scenarios.
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
Unerwarteter Verlust (UL) (unexpected loss)
The loss above expectation at the scenario confidence, delivered by the IRB formula \(K=[LGD\cdot PD_{cond}-LGD\cdot PD]\). This is the reported credit risk.
→ Module 5; CRR Art. 153/154
Excess / Shortfall (excess / shortfall of provisions)
The difference between portfolio EL and accounting Wertberichtigungen (Vorsorgen); a provisioning surplus adds to, a shortfall subtracts from, the Deckungsmasse.
→ Module 5/8; Leitfaden Ch.5.1/6
IRB-Ansatz (internal-ratings-based approach)
The Basel/CRR approach computing capital from internal PD/LGD/EAD via the Vasicek/ASRF formula; the engine for credit-risk UL in the RTFA.
→ Module 5; CRR Art. 142 ff.
CRR Art. 153 (corporate IRB formula)
The risk-weight formula for Corporates, FI, Sovereigns, LRG and CIU, with a PD-dependent asset correlation \(R(PD)\) and a maturity adjustment.
→ Module 5; CRR (EU) 575/2013 Art. 153
CRR Art. 154 (retail IRB formula)
The retail risk-weight formula (Retail selbständig/unselbständig): same conditional-PD core but with fixed correlations (0,03–0,16; QRRE 0,04; mortgages 0,15) and no maturity adjustment.
→ Module 5; CRR Art. 154
PD (Ausfallwahrscheinlichkeit) (probability of default)
The probability a borrower defaults within one year, assigned by Ratingstufe via the master scale; a primary IRB input.
→ Module 5; CRR Art. 160; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
PD PiT vs. TtC (point-in-time vs. through-the-cycle PD)
TtC PD averages over the cycle (used as the base rating PD); PiT PD reflects current conditions and is what the FX-elasticity regression stresses.
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
FX-Elastizität (1,9) (FX elasticity of PD)
The estimated elasticity of the PiT PD to local-currency depreciation: 1,9 (190 %), an ÖRS choice (RBI-Retail proxy). \(PD_{PiT}=PD_{TtC}(1+1{,}9\,\Delta_{fx})\). The Leitfaden's "273 %" worked example is an erratum.
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1 ÖRS choice
LGD (loss given default)
Loss as a share of EAD given default. Regulatory unsecured 45 %; ÖRS pooled estimates 45,5 % Corporate, 54,4 % Retail selbständig, 55,8 % Retail unselbständig; covered bonds 12,5 %; defaults 80 % (5,1) / 100 % (5,2).
→ Module 5/15; CRR Art. 161; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
EAD (exposure at default)
The exposure expected to be outstanding at default; the multiplier turning the per-unit capital \(K\) into euro UL and RWA (\(RWA=K\cdot12{,}5\cdot EAD\)).
→ Module 5; CRR Art. 166
M (effektive Restlaufzeit 2,5) (effective maturity)
Effective residual maturity in the maturity adjustment. Fixed at 2,5 years for IRB-formula credits (so the adjustment = 1); 1 year for "nur Wertpapiere" to avoid double-counting downgrade risk.
→ Module 5; CRR Art. 162; Leitfaden Ch.5.1 ÖRS choice
Asset-Korrelation (R) (asset correlation)
The share of a firm's variance driven by the systematic factor. Corporates: \(R=0{,}12\frac{1-e^{-50PD}}{1-e^{-50}}+0{,}24(1-\frac{1-e^{-50PD}}{1-e^{-50}})\), interpolating 0,24 (low PD) to 0,12 (high PD).
→ Module 5; CRR Art. 153
Maturity Adjustment (maturity adjustment)
The factor \(\frac{1+(M-2{,}5)b}{1-1{,}5b}\) with \(b(PD)=(0{,}11852-0{,}05478\ln PD)^2\); normalised to 1 at \(M=2{,}5\), which is why the fixed M makes it vanish for corporates.
→ Module 5; CRR Art. 153
Vasicek / ASRF (asymptotic single-risk-factor model)
Vasicek (2002) / Gordy (2003) one-factor portfolio limit that delivers portfolio invariance, letting credit risk be computed loan-by-loan and summed. The basis of the CRR formula.
→ Module 5; Vasicek (2002), Gordy (2003)
Merton-Modell (Merton firm-value model)
Merton (1974): a firm defaults when latent asset value falls below liabilities, \(PD=\Phi(c)\Rightarrow c=\Phi^{-1}(PD)\). The microfoundation of the IRB conditional PD.
→ Module 5; Merton (1974)
Gaussian Copula (Gaussian copula)
The dependence structure linking borrowers' latent normals through one systematic factor: \(V_i=\sqrt{\rho}X+\sqrt{1-\rho}\varepsilon_i\). Its single factor is why the model cannot capture sector/regional concentration.
→ Module 5; Vasicek (2002)
Risikoposition (risk position)
= Exposure − interne Sicherheiten per customer, floored at zero; the netted basis to which the credit-risk formula is applied.
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
Exposure (exposure)
Balance (book/market value) + weighted off-balance (Haftungswert: letters of credit, derivatives, repos) + weighted undrawn committed lines + weighted (≥20 %) undrawn internal lines. Netting/CSA cash reduces the Saldo; provisions are not deducted.
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
Forderungsklassen (exposure classes)
Corporates, Financial Institutions, Sovereigns, Retail selbständig, Retail unselbständig, LRG (Local & Regional Governments) and CIU (Collective Investment Undertakings / funds). They route to Art. 153 vs. 154.
→ Module 5; CRR Art. 147; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
Fremdwährungskreditrisiko (FX credit risk add-on)
An add-on scaling FX-loan exposure up by the scenario-scaled annual FX volatility (3-yr history), treating the uplift as unsecured. FI and Sovereigns get no FX credit risk (materiality / consolidation).
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
Konzentrationsrisiko (concentration risk)
The extra risk from single-name lumpiness that the infinitely-granular IRB formula ignores; captured by the Granularitätsaufschlag.
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
Granularitätsaufschlag (granularity surcharge)
The gap between real-portfolio UL and an infinitely granular portfolio's UL; surcharge = UL·HHI, new UL = UL·(1+HHI).
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
HHI (Herfindahl-Hirschman-Index) (Herfindahl-Hirschman index)
\(HHI=hhi_{21}+hhi_{22}\), \(hhi_{21}=\sum s_i^2\) over the top Corporates (max 110 open positions, per GvK), \(hhi_{22}=s_m(1-S_m)\) tail correction; \(s_i\)= open single / total open corporate position. \(HHI\in[1/n,1]\).
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
Masterskala (master rating scale)
The 10-step master scale mapping Ratingstufen to midpoint PDs (geometric mean of bounds): 0,5→0,0533 %, 1,0→0,12 %, 2,5→1,3669 %, 4,5→35,0315 %, 5,x→100 %, NR→35,0315 %. FI/Sov/LRG/CIU use RBI-group PDs.
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
Migrationsrisiko (migration risk)
The risk of rating downgrades short of default, assessed via annual rating-model migration matrices (MMATRIX); for "nur Wertpapiere" it is carried in credit-spread/market risk instead.
→ Module 5/6; Leitfaden Ch.5.1
Tilgungsträger (repayment vehicle)
An investment vehicle (e.g. life policy, fund) accumulating to repay a bullet FX loan; its shortfall risk compounds the FX credit risk on such loans.
→ Module 5; Leitfaden Ch.5.1

Marktrisiko

Zinsrisiko im Bankbuch (IRRBB) (interest-rate risk in the banking book)
Parametric VaR on gap present values per maturity band & currency over a 250-day holding period; Risk = Barwert − simulierter Barwert, gaps discounted at spot vs. (rate·vola).
→ Module 6; Leitfaden Ch.5.5; EBA/GL/2018/02
200-bp-Shift (+200 bp parallel shift)
A +200 bp parallel curve shift applied to currencies with negative rates so the log-volatility method yields positive logs; an ÖRS modelling fix.
→ Module 6; Leitfaden Ch.5.5 ÖRS choice
Währungsrisiko / offene Devisenposition (FX risk / open currency position)
Per currency, Risk = Position · Vola(holding period) · percentile(95–99,9 %). Undiversified = sign-neutral sum; diversified uses the correlation matrix.
→ Module 6; Leitfaden Ch.5.5
Preisrisiko (price risk)
Trading-book price risk = own-funds charge (position/FX/commodity) minus FX charge, scaled ×0,71 (Problemfall) / ×1,33 (Extremfall); banking-book equities treated like Beteiligungsrisiko, fund/index holdings via VaR (e.g. ATX).
→ Module 6; Leitfaden Ch.5.5
Spreadrisiko (Credit-Spread-VaR) (credit-spread risk)
VaR on "nur Wertpapiere"/"nur Covered Bonds" by Forderungsklasse × Ratingklasse, using relative bond volatilities (Markit Overall, 3-yr) scaled to 250 days; Risk = PV2 − PV1, diversified via a rating-class correlation matrix.
→ Module 6; Leitfaden Ch.5.5
Mischzinssatz (blended discount rate)
= (IRS+CDS)·(1−Primäreinlagenanteil) + SPI·Primäreinlagenanteil; the discount rate used in the FLVaR. Stressed by adding CDS- and SPI-volatility at 99,9 %.
→ Module 7; Leitfaden Ch.5.7

CVA & Liquidität

CVA-Risiko (0,71 / 1,33) (credit-valuation-adjustment risk)
The CVA own-funds requirement (PosNr 7516000, EM-weight 8 %) scaled by a normal-quantile ratio: Problemfall ×0,71 = 1,6449/2,3263; Extremfall ×1,33 = 3,0902/2,3263. ÖRS scaling on a regulatory charge.
→ Module 6; CRR Title VI; Leitfaden Ch.5.2 ÖRS choice
Liquiditätsrisiko (liquidity risk)
Problemfall: measured per Leitfaden Liquidität with NO Deckungsmassen charge. Extremfall: structural/liquidity-price risk via FLVaR. A simplified factor approach exists for small institutions (full-deposit, 1 % indicator, 100 bp).
→ Module 7; Leitfaden Ch.5.7
FLVaR (Funding-Liquidity-VaR) (funding-liquidity VaR)
Simplified variance-covariance VaR on open non-cumulative liquidity gaps >1yr (bands >5yr netted), confidence 99,9 %, holding 250 days; counts only when there is a funding need. Risk = PV2 − PV1, diversified via a CDS correlation matrix.
→ Module 7; Leitfaden Ch.5.7
Primäreinlagen (primary deposits)
Customer deposits/savings/sight balances plus own retail/non-retail issues (excl. equity and liquidity reserve). Primäreinlagenanteil = Σ Primäreinlagen>1yr / Σ Gesamtrefi>1yr; interbank = total refi − Primäreinlagen.
→ Module 7; Leitfaden Ch.5.7

Beteiligungs- & sonstige Risiken

Beteiligungsrisiko (participation / equity-holding risk)
Risk on equity participations: Exposure = Verkehrswert, Risk = Verkehrswert · Risikofaktor (by rating & segment). Sub-risks: dividend default, write-down, disposal loss, top-up, hidden-reserve reduction. NO diversification — single risks are summed. "3× rule": Extremfall ≈ 3× Problemfall.
→ Module 12; Leitfaden Ch.5.4
Teilwertabschreibung (TWA) (write-down to lower value)
A write-down of a participation's carrying value to a lower fair value; the Teilwertabschreibungsrisiko is one sub-risk of Beteiligungsrisiko.
→ Module 12; Leitfaden Ch.5.4
Veräußerungsverlustrisiko (disposal-loss risk)
The risk of realising a loss on selling a participation below its carrying value; a Beteiligungsrisiko sub-component set at 95 %/99,9 %.
→ Module 12; Leitfaden Ch.5.4
Nachschusspflicht (top-up / additional contribution obligation)
An obligation to inject further capital into a participation; the Nachschussverpflichtungsrisiko captures potential top-up calls.
→ Module 12; Leitfaden Ch.5.4
Stille Reserven / Lasten (hidden reserves / hidden charges)
The difference between market/Verkehrswert and book value on securities and participations; positive (reserves) add to the Deckungsmasse, negative (Lasten) subtract. Reduction of hidden reserves is itself a Beteiligungs sub-risk.
→ Module 8/12; Leitfaden Ch.6/5.4
Ausübungswahrscheinlichkeit (exercise probability)
A CCF-analogue probability adding off-balance Eventualverpflichtungen to participation exposure by rating: 0,5:0 %, 1,0:5 %, 1,5:10 %, 2,0:20 %, 2,5:30 %, 3,0/3,5:50 %, 4,0/4,5:80 %, 5,x:100 %.
→ Module 12; Leitfaden Ch.5.4
Sektorbeteiligung (sector / RBI participation)
Holdings in sector entities; e.g. the RBI participation rated A2 (1,0) carries a 10 % Problemfall / 30 % Extremfall risk factor.
→ Module 12; Leitfaden Ch.5.4
Länderrisiko (country / transfer risk)
A cross-border add-on for transfer/convertibility risk not captured by rating ceilings; computed like credit risk on internal data before collateral, by sovereign-rating PD, UL only, excluding defaults (5,x).
→ Module 11; Leitfaden Ch.5.3
Makroökonomisches Risiko (macroeconomic risk)
Applied only to credit risk: a ~2 % GDP decline raises PDs ~25 % and LGDs ~5 %; macro risk = recomputed credit risk − original credit risk (both EL and UL).
→ Module 13; Leitfaden Ch.5.8 ÖRS choice
Risiko aus Großkrediten (GvK) (large-exposure risk)
Assessed at Gruppe verbundener Kunden (GvK, OeNB customer structure); largest exposures related to R-IPS CET1, excluding R-IPS-member companies/banks/issues.
→ Module 11; CRR Part Four; Leitfaden Ch.5.9
Gruppe verbundener Kunden (GvK) (group of connected clients)
A set of customers treated as a single risk because of control/economic interdependence (OeNB customer structure); the unit for large-exposure and HHI computations.
→ Module 5/11; CRR Art. 4(1)(39)
Puffer sonstige Risiken (5 %) (other-risks buffer)
A 5 % add-on on the quantified risks in both scenarios, approximating strategic/reputational/business-model/systemic/model risk; partly justified because future operating income is not counted in the Extremfall Deckungsmasse.
→ Module 13; Leitfaden Ch.5.10 ÖRS choice
Fremdwährungs-Eigenmittelrisiko (FX own-funds risk)
Problemfall only: an FX rise raises the loan Saldo and thus the RWA base; the Risikoposition is scaled by FX vola (proxy 15 % Problemfall / 30 % Extremfall), and 8 % of that is taken as the own-funds risk. Not applied in the Extremfall.
→ Module 13; Leitfaden Ch.5.10 ÖRS choice
Operationelles Risiko (5 % / 15 %) (operational risk)
Extremfall = 15 % of Betriebserträge (Basisindikatoransatz-anchored). Problemfall = 5,0 %, derived by scaling 15 % from 99,9 % to 95 % under a ⅓ normal + ⅔ lognormal mix. Includes IKT and AML risk.
→ Module 9; Leitfaden Ch.5.6
IKT-Risiko (ICT / IT risk)
Information- and communication-technology risk, treated as part of operational risk in the RTFA (alongside AML/Geldwäsche).
→ Module 9; Leitfaden Ch.5.6; DORA context

Deckungsmassen & Eigenmittel

Eigenmittel (CET1 / AT1 / T2) (own funds: CET1, AT1, Tier 2)
Regulatory capital tiers: Common Equity Tier 1, Additional Tier 1, Tier 2. CET1 above the legal requirement (incl. P2R) feeds Freies Kapital; anrechenbare Eigenmittel feed the Extremfall Deckungsmasse.
→ Module 1/8; CRR Part Two; Leitfaden Ch.6
Freies Kapital (free capital)
CET1 above the legal requirement (incl. P2R if set) minus Neubewertungsreserve, subject to a €5m legal floor; a Deckungsmasse component.
→ Module 8; Leitfaden Ch.6/8.1
Freier Jahresüberschuss / Mindestausschüttung (free annual surplus / minimum distribution)
Budgeted Jahresüberschuss + Gewinnvortrag − Mindestausschüttung (quarterly haircut 85/85/90/100 % Q1–Q4); the earnings part of the Problemfall Deckungsmasse.
→ Module 8; Leitfaden Ch.6/8.1
Neubewertungsreserve (revaluation reserve)
A revaluation reserve deducted from Freies Kapital and subtracted again in the Extremfall Deckungsmasse to avoid double-counting unrealised value.
→ Module 8; Leitfaden Ch.6/8.1
Haftsummenzuschlag (liability-sum surcharge)
A cooperative members' supplementary-liability component that can be recognised as loss-absorbing capacity in the cooperative context.
→ Module 8; Leitfaden Ch.6
Kapitalerhaltungspuffer (capital conservation buffer)
A CET1 buffer above minimum requirements (melder PosNr 8127010) included in the Problemfall traffic-light thresholds.
→ Module 1/8; CRD Art. 129; Leitfaden Ch.8.1
Antizyklischer Puffer (countercyclical buffer)
A time-varying CET1 buffer (melder PosNr 8127030) raised in credit booms; part of the buffer stack in the Problemfall green threshold.
→ Module 1; CRD Art. 130; Leitfaden Ch.8.1
Systemrisikopuffer (SyRB) (systemic risk buffer)
A CET1 buffer against systemic/macroprudential risk (melder PosNr 8128000); part of the buffer stack in the RTFA thresholds.
→ Module 1; CRD Art. 133; Leitfaden Ch.8.1
Leverage Ratio (leverage ratio)
A non-risk-based ratio of Tier 1 to total exposure (≥3 %); a backstop to the risk-weighted requirements in the regulatory architecture.
→ Module 1; CRR Art. 429
P2R / P2G (Pillar 2 requirement / guidance)
SREP-set additional own-funds: the binding Pillar-2 Requirement (P2R, included in the legal requirement for Freies Kapital) and the non-binding Pillar-2 Guidance (P2G).
→ Module 1/8; CRD Art. 104a/104b; Leitfaden Ch.4
Gesamtrisikobetrag (total risk exposure amount / RWA)
Total risk-weighted exposure (melder PosNr 7980000); the denominator of regulatory capital ratios used to set the RTFA thresholds.
→ Module 8; CRR Art. 92; Leitfaden Ch.8.1

Ausfall & Parameter

Ausfalldefinition (Art. 178) (definition of default)
Default triggers: (a) unlikely-to-pay; (b) >90 days past due on a material obligation. Mapping: 5,0 = >90 days overdue; 5,1 = other default reasons; 5,2 = insolvency or write-off.
→ Module 10; CRR Art. 178; Leitfaden Ch.10.2
Erheblichkeitsschwelle (100 / 500 + 1 %) (materiality threshold)
Per Leitlinie (EU) 2020/978: absolute €100 retail / €500 non-retail AND relative 1 % of total on-balance exposures (excl. equity). Insolvency and Fälligstellung have no threshold (≥€1).
→ Module 10; Leitlinie (EU) 2020/978; Leitfaden Ch.10.2
Re-aging / Wohlverhaltensperiode (Cure Period) (re-aging / probation / cure period)
Cure requires ≥12 months sustained improvement; "re-aging" is a two-stage process with ≥90-day probation (RZ 71 EBA/GL/2016/07) and ≤30-day arrears at the 2nd check. Loss-bearing default reasons cannot end merely because the customer pays again.
→ Module 10; EBA/GL/2016/07; Leitfaden Ch.10.2
Wertberichtigungen / Vorsorgen (provisions / impairments)
Accounting provisions against credit losses; compared with portfolio EL to form the excess/shortfall in the Deckungsmasse. They are not deducted from the Saldo.
→ Module 5/8; Leitfaden Ch.5.1/6
Betriebserträge (operating income)
The operating-income base for operational risk: 15 % (Extremfall) / 5 % (Problemfall) of Betriebserträge, anchored to the Basisindikatoransatz.
→ Module 9; Leitfaden Ch.5.6

Daten, Reporting & Aufsichtsrecht

COREP / FINREP / VERA (supervisory reporting frameworks)
The harmonised prudential (COREP) and financial (FINREP) reporting frameworks plus the Austrian VERA returns; source data for the Deckungsmasse (Belege 7, 12, 15, 80/81, FINREP/FINUS).
→ Module 8/16; Leitfaden Ch.6/8
R1-Format (R1 data format)
The delivery format per the "ÖRS Datenspezifikation" used to submit RTFA inputs each quarter, on both an unconsolidated and consolidated basis.
→ Module 16; Leitfaden Ch.8/9
BAT (Bankarbeitstage) (bank working days)
The reporting deadline unit: results are due 10 BAT after quarter-end, aligned to the OeNB Meldekalender; reporting address srg-office@rbinternational.com.
→ Module 16; Leitfaden Ch.8/9
SOT (Supervisory Outlier Test) (supervisory outlier test)
The IRRBB outlier tests (EVE/NII) flagging banks whose interest-rate risk exceeds supervisory thresholds; reported as an RTFA Kennziffer.
→ Module 6/16; EBA/GL/2018/02; SOT RTS
Kennziffern (key ratios)
RTFA monitoring ratios: Cost-Income-Ratio (CIR), Loan/Deposit, NPE ratio, Asset Encumbrance and the IRRBB outlier/SOT tests.
→ Module 16; Leitfaden Ch.8/9
CIR (Cost-Income-Ratio) (cost-income ratio)
Operating expenses over operating income; an efficiency Kennziffer monitored alongside the RTFA.
→ Module 16; Leitfaden Ch.9
NPE-Quote (non-performing exposure ratio)
The share of non-performing exposures in total exposure; an asset-quality Kennziffer in the reporting pack.
→ Module 16; EBA NPE GL; Leitfaden Ch.9
Asset Encumbrance (asset encumbrance)
The share of assets pledged/encumbered (e.g. for covered bonds or repos); a liquidity/structural Kennziffer monitored in the RTFA.
→ Module 16; Leitfaden Ch.9
Output Floor (output floor)
CRR III floor limiting modelled RWAs to 72,5 % of the standardised RWAs (phased to 2030); supersedes parts of the Leitfaden's IRB-based view. Verify against current EUR-Lex/EBA.
→ Module 16; CRR III; regulatory
FRTB (Fundamental Review of the Trading Book)
The revised CRR III market-risk framework replacing the current trading-book own-funds charge the Leitfaden relies on; EU application from 1 Jan 2025 (FRTB own-funds reporting delayed).
→ Module 16; CRR III; regulatory
SMA (Standardised Measurement Approach, op risk)
The CRR III operational-risk method = Business Indicator Component × Internal Loss Multiplier, replacing BIA/TSA/AMA; supersedes the Leitfaden's BIA-style 15 %/5 % approach.
→ Module 16; CRR III; regulatory
DGSD / ESAEG (Einlagensicherung) (deposit guarantee schemes directive / Austrian act)
EU DGSD 2014/49/EU and Austrian ESAEG: coverage €100.000 per depositor per institution, target level 0,8 % of covered deposits. A DGS pays out depositors, distinct from the R-IPS (Art. 113(7)) which keeps members solvent. Verify current Austrian arrangement.
→ Module 17; DGSD 2014/49/EU; ESAEG
