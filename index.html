📘 What this course is

A complete, self-paced reconstruction of the ÖRS Leitfaden Früherkennung (Version 09/2024) — the methodology your unit runs every quarter. Every risk figure is built from first principles, anchored in the CRR article it derives from, and contrasted with the pragmatic ÖRS modelling choices layered on top. Taught in English, with every technical term kept in German and glossed once.

⚖️ The one rule that runs through everything

For every fixed number you will meet — M = 2,5, LGD = 45%, the FX elasticity 1,9, the CVA scalars 0,71 / 1,33, the op-risk 5% / 15%, the 5% "sonstige Risiken" buffer — the course tells you whether it is a regulatory value the CRR forces, or an ÖRS modelling decision made for proportionality, and what that choice trades off. That distinction is what you will defend to the Aufsichtsrat.

Where the Leitfaden (Sept 2024) may lag current rules — notably CRR III / Basel IV for op-risk and CVA, and DGSD/ESAEG deposit-guarantee specifics — the course flags it explicitly for you to verify. This is an educational reconstruction, not an official ÖRS document.
